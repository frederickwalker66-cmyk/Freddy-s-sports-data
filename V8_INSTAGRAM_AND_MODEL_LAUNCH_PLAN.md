## Two parallel lanes

### Lane A: model skill (first priority)
1. Authenticate original numeric source rights/values; independently crosscheck NBA game results, minutes, injuries and prices. Do not use suspected scrambled SportsDataIO trial values.
2. Archive pregame event snapshots with authentic event IDs, source publication times, model training cutoffs and immutable receipts.

4. Evaluate chronological holdouts and show Brier, log loss, calibration, market no-vig baseline and uncertainty, including failed runs and excluded games.

6. Expand to NFL, MLB, NHL, NCAAF, NCAAB, MLS and UEFA as distinct models. Keep regulation vs overtime and three-way draw rules separate.

## Verified performance posting policy
- Every published prediction needs a pre-event timestamp, bookmaker, exact line, event/market, model version, source receipt, model probability, actual settlement and final result, including losses/voids.
- Do NOT publish research candidates as certified +EV picks.
- Do NOT calculate profit or winning percentage unless all denominators and complete recorded outcomes are available.
- Mark hypothetical examples unambiguously; never label simulated examples as money won.
- Respect local betting advertising rules, platform disclosure requirements and age restrictions; keep educational posts non-promotional while validations remain blocked.
- Always allow viewers to inspect how the record was assembled.
